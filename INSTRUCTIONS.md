
#Prototype Submission Guidelines

Please read all the instructions carefully before submitting your prototype.

**1. Submission Deadline**

- The repository will remain open for submissions until 8 October, 11:50 AM.
- The repository will be closed immediately after the deadline.
- Once the repository is closed, participants will lose access to the GitHub submission repository.
- Make sure your final submission is pushed before the deadline.

**2. Working Prototype — Mandatory**

Your submitted prototype must be fully runnable and ready for demonstration.

Before submitting, make sure that:

- Participants are responsible for ensuring that the prototype works correctly before the submission deadline.
- The submitted repository must contain all the necessary files required to run the prototype.
- The submitted version is the final version that you intend to present.

A prototype that cannot be run or demonstrated may result in disqualification.

**3. Backup Demonstration Video**

Every team is strongly required to keep a recorded video of their working prototype/demo prepared on their side.

The video will act as a backup in case of:

- Technical issues during the live demonstration
- Prototype execution errors
- Internet/system-related problems
- Other unexpected technical difficulties

Participants should ensure that the backup video clearly demonstrates the functionality of the prototype.

**4. Presentation Guidelines**

- Each team will be given a maximum of 8 minutes to present their prototype.
- The presentation should be planned and rehearsed beforehand.
- Exceeding the 8-minute limit will result in deduction of marks.
- Participants are advised to keep the presentation concise and focus on:

1. Problem Statement
2. Proposed Solution
3. Key Features
4. Prototype Demonstration
5. Innovation / Impact

**5. Terms & Conditions**

- By submitting the prototype, participants agree to follow all the rules and guidelines of Organizing Scratch to Hack.
- Participants are expected to maintain originality, proper conduct, and fair participation.
- Any violation of the event rules or terms and conditions may lead to disqualification.

All participants are expected to:

- Maintain respectful and professional communication.
- Respect other participants, organizers, mentors, and evaluators.
- Avoid plagiarism, cheating, harassment, or inappropriate behaviour.
- Submit their own work and provide appropriate credit for third-party resources when required.
-Follow all instructions provided by the organizing team.
